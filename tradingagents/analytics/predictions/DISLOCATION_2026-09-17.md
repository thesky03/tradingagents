## The case against the top call, stated plainly

ACN scores well because forward growth (~5.5% FY27 consensus) sits just
above trailing (~4.0%), margins expanded 20bps, and cash conversion is
1.39×. All four are thin margins of safety, and three things cut against it:

1. **Bookings are contracting.** Q3 FY26 new bookings $19.3B vs $19.7B,
   −2% USD / −3% local currency. FY2025 full-year bookings were already
   −1%. That is the same *sign* as CoStar, the cleanest trap in the
   research — though −2% is a long way from CoStar's −26%. The screen
   reports this as a caution and does **not** score it, because the case
   set had too few bookings figures to test whether it separates.
2. **The forward growth figure is an analyst aggregate, not guidance.**
   Accenture has not issued FY2027 guidance; the call is 1 Oct 2026.
   The +1.5% acceleration is the thinnest input in the table and it
   could invert on that call.
3. **The trajectory reading is 73, not 90.** This is a marginal
   dislocation, not an obvious one.

Against that: an 8.3% owner yield (3.3% dividend + 6.4% buyback − 1.4%
SBC) that pays whether or not the market agrees, and +70% if the
multiple merely returns to its own median.

## The residual the model cannot fix

Cognizant scored 60.0 against Accenture's 66.2 at their 2026 troughs.
Same sector, same narrative, same catalyst day. ACN recovered 60%; CTSH
fell another 44%. What separated them — $5.1B of GenAI bookings, 85,000
AI professionals against an 80,000 target, and a buyback raised to
$7.5B — was disclosed *after* the bottom. A trajectory screen cannot
see a disclosure that has not happened yet, so it buys both.

**The 8% cap and the three tranches exist because of exactly that.**

## How to grade this

ACN and ADBE against SPY from 17 Sep 2026, at 3, 6 and 12 months. The
traps (FI, DECK, LULU) should underperform over the same window. If
LULU — down 56% with forward growth −6.1% and guidance cut twice — is
the best performer of the six, the screen is wrong in the way that
matters most.
