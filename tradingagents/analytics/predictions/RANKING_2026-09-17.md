## Verification cost 13.5 SKY points on average

Not noise around the truth — bias in one direction. ADBE 84.2 → 69.0,
BKNG 78.6 → 65.6, SPGI 68.6 → 53.6, GILD 74.2 → 49.6, UTHR 75.2 → 58.6.
Every archetype estimate was optimistic.

The commodity and emerging-market names that topped the estimated
ranking fell furthest, for one reason: their owner yields are
**variable payouts at a cyclical peak**, not rates.

- VALE's dividend is a formula: 30% of adjusted EBITDA less sustaining
  capex, semi-annual. 47.3.

  has already said extraordinary dividends are unlikely. 40.7.

Brazil votes in October 2026. PBR, VALE and STNE all carry that.

## Two systematic data gaps

**SBC was not sourced for 15 of 17.** It lives in cash-flow-statement
detail pages, which is exactly what this environment's proxy blocks.
Dividends and buybacks add to owner yield and SBC subtracts, so the
error has one direction: too good. `owner_yield_is_complete()` now
flags it rather than banking the difference.

**Forward revenue growth was not sourced for 7 of 17** — oil, gas and
mining majors guide production and capex, not revenue. That is the
input the backtest found most predictive (AUC 0.79), so its absence in
the commodity names is not a small gap.

## Every name reads AVOID

The entry rule returns AVOID on all seventeen, because the Behavior
pillar scores momentum and most of these sit below their highs. That is
the same circularity the dislocation screen was built to break, showing
up in the main path. The ranking answers *which of these is the better
business at the better price*; it does not claim the tape agrees.

## How to grade this

The ten against SPY at 3, 6 and 12 months. The specific falsifiers:
if BKNG (−8.9pt growth deceleration) beats ADBE (+1.3pt), the strongest
signal in the backtest did not hold here. If the unscoreable three would
have beaten the ten, the completeness gate cost more than it saved.
