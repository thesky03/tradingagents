# Five mid/small caps for a decade — frozen 17 Sep 2026

Universe expanded by 149 mid/small caps (median $5.5B, 118 under $10B).
Fourteen shortlisted on business character, all fourteen researched name
by name. Frozen before outcomes are known.

## What "confidence" can honestly mean here

A 5x in ten years is **17.46% a year**. Calibrating the simulator at
constant quality:

| demonstrated growth | P(5x in 10y) |
|---|---|
| 14% | 12% |
| 18% | 19% |
| 22% | 28% |
| 28% | 42% |
| 35% | 60% |

**Even a 28% grower reaches only 42%.** Nobody can have confidence in a
specific 5x at this horizon. What is available is whether the required
rate sits *inside* what the business has already demonstrated — the
**gap** — and whether the downside is bounded.

Note also that P(5x) rewards fragility: holding growth at 22% and only
removing the moat raises it from 25% to 34%, because a wider cone throws
more paths past the threshold. So nothing here is ranked on P(5x). It is
ranked on the gap, with the 10th-percentile floor reported beside it.

### 1. PLMR — the only one where everything points the same way
Revenue $247.8M → $876.0M over five years, a 37% CAGR with **no
deceleration** (FY25 accelerated to +58%). ROE 23.6% GAAP / 25.9%
adjusted, combined ratio 76.9%. Guidance raised **three times in 2026**;
15th consecutive beat. Founder-CEO. **Share count has shrunk over five
years while revenue tripled** — the only name in the set where that is
true. $3.65B → $18B is the most modest ask of the five.
*Threat:* the model rests on ceding catastrophe risk cheaply. A hard
reinsurance market compresses it regardless of underwriting skill.

### 2. TOST — the deceleration is real and still leaves room
Growth 107/60/42/28/**24%** — textbook steady deceleration, and the gap
is still +6.6 at 24%. First full GAAP-profitable year: $342M net income,
$608M FCF. **Share count has flattened** (605M → 602M) with a buyback
explicitly offsetting comp, and SBC is 4.0% of revenue, lowest of any
software name researched. Founder-CEO. ~180K of a claimed 1.4M
locations. AI is a *documented* tailwind here — pilot customers recorded
an 8% sales lift — and a general model cannot replace a POS terminal
and a merchant-acquiring relationship.
*Threat:* most gross profit is fintech, not software. A decade of
take-rate compression hits the part that funds everything else.

### 3. IESC — best numbers, one unresolved question that decides it
Revenue $1.54B → $3.371B, +40% in the latest quarter, operating income
+60% on that, backlog $4.5B and **+91% since fiscal year-end**, ROE
36.5%, no debt, insiders own 57%.
**The deciding unknown: FY2025 operating cash flow of $92M against
$306M of net income — 0.30x conversion.** That is the weakest
conversion in anything researched today and it is the exact signature
that marked the value traps in the backtest. The source is single and
weak, so it is either wrong or it is the most important fact about the
company. *Do not size this before that number is confirmed.*
*Threat:* the +40% and the backlog are one bet — data centres. A
hyperscaler capex pause removes the growth and the margin together.

### 4. APPF — highest quality, least room
ROIC 38%, zero debt, $251M net cash, FCF $236M on $140.9M net income,
guidance raised twice in 2026, share count flat. The gap is only +1.3,
so this one needs its current pace held almost exactly.
*Threat:* units on platform grow 8% while revenue grows 19%. The
difference is monetisation per unit, and that has a ceiling.

### 5. MEDP — included *against* its own guidance, deliberately
On the FY26 guide of +12.5% the gap is −3.0 and the verdict is NEEDS
RE-ACCELERATION. It is here because the **leading indicator disagrees
with the guide**: net new business awards +28.2%, book-to-bill 1.13x.
The backtest found leading-indicator direction to be the single
strongest signal (AUC 0.79 for growth deceleration, and the loudest trap
marker was CoStar growing revenue 18% while bookings fell 26%). Applied
symmetrically, a company whose bookings are inflecting up while its
guide is conservative is the mirror image of that.
Supporting: founder-CEO since 1992, **share count shrinking ~4%/yr** —
best trajectory in the group — net cash $497M, FCF $681.9M against
$451.1M net income, SBC only 1.4% of revenue.
*Threat:* 81% of revenue from small biopharma. This is a levered bet on
the biotech funding cycle, not a diversified CRO.

## KNSL was excluded, and why that matters

Kinsale has a +3.1 gap, 28.9% ROE, a 75.5% combined ratio, a
founder-CEO and a shrinking share count. It would otherwise be on this
list.

**Q2 2026 gross written premium declined 5.0% — the first shrinkage.**
Revenue still grew 16.8% because earned premium and investment income
lag. That is precisely the CoStar shape: the reported line growing while
the leading indicator contracts. Having built a screen that calls this
the loudest trap signature, including the name anyway would make the
screen decorative. Its owner yield is also incomplete — SBC not
sourceable.

## Rejected, with the reason

- **DUOL** (gap −11.8) — DAU +23% while **bookings +8%**. Engagement
  compounding three times faster than money; revenue guide maintained
  while margin guide raised. A margin story, not a growth story.
- **CSWI** (−11.1) — +33% headline growth is acquired; organic is 5.9%.
  Net income *fell* 18%, FCF conversion 49% of EBITDA, 2.55x leverage.
- **SPSC** (−10.1) — decelerated 18% → 5–6%, guidance cut, share count
  rising 3.4%, bought two businesses and divested part within 18 months.
- **DSGX** (−5.1) — 12% growth against a 17.1% requirement. Profit grows
  32–36% on margin expansion, which has a ceiling. CEO owns 0.052% and
  is paid $10.4M.
- **KVYO** — blocked on returns below cost of capital. Worst dilution
  researched: +25% shares in two years against SBC at 13% of revenue.
- **NTRA** — blocked on the same test, and at $50.6B a 5x means $253B.
- **CYBR** — acquired by Palo Alto Networks, closed 11 Feb 2026,
  delisted. Verified independently.
- **GSHD** — every price source conflicts across April–September. No
  reliable price, no valuation. Growth decelerating 38/25/20/16%.

## How to grade this

Each name against the Russell 2000 at 1, 3 and 5 years, and the gap
re-computed every quarter. The specific falsifiers:
- **PLMR** fails if GWP growth drops below ~15% for two quarters.
- **TOST** fails if location adds fall below ~7,000/quarter or the
  fintech take rate compresses.
- **IESC** fails immediately if the 0.30x cash conversion is real.
- **APPF** fails if revenue growth converges toward its 8% unit growth.
- **MEDP** fails if book-to-bill drops below 1.0 — the whole reason it
  is on this list instead of KNSL.

If KNSL outperforms all five, the leading-indicator rule that excluded
it is wrong, and that is the most valuable thing this record can teach.
